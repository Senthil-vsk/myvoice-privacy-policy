Privacy Policy for myvoice

Effective Date: September 29, 2026
Powered by G-One

1. Introduction

Welcome to myvoice. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the myvoice mobile application (the "App") and related digital governance, public safety, and emergency response services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide life-saving SOS functionalities and seamless governance services, the App collects the following types of data:

  • Personal Information & Digital Identity: Name, contact details, and authenticated digital identity credentials via our digital identity gateway.
  • Location Data: Precise and approximate location data (GPS), including background location when an active SOS or emergency ticket is triggered.
  • Media and Files: Photos, videos, and audio recordings captured via the App as real-time incident payloads for grievance redressal and emergency response.
  • Health and Telemetry Data: Vital signs and related health telemetry collected via integrated hardware wearables during an emergency event.
  • Device and Usage Data: Device identifiers, operating system version, and system logs to maintain a tamper-proof audit trail.

3. How We Use Your Information

We collect and process your data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Emergency Response (SOS): To dispatch precise location, health telemetry, and live media to emergency responders and government authorities during an active SOS trigger.
  • Governance & Grievance Redressal: To log, process, and track your submitted municipal issues via our immutable, ticket-based governance engine.
  • Identity Verification: To securely authenticate users using cryptographic tokenization with zero local identity caching.
  • Platform Security: To monitor system health, prevent abuse, and maintain role-based access controls.

4. App Permissions and Prominent Disclosures

The App requires the following permissions to function. We only request these permissions when strictly necessary:

  • Location (Foreground and Background): Required to broadcast your real-time coordinates to emergency responders during an SOS event and to accurately map municipal grievances. Background location is only accessed when an active SOS session is running.
  • Camera and Microphone: Required to capture photos, videos, and audio evidence for incident reporting and SOS telemetry.
  • Body Sensors (Health Data): Required to transmit life-saving vital telemetry from connected wearables to responders during emergencies.
  • Storage / Files: Required to temporarily cache and upload incident payloads to our secure cloud archival.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We operate on a zero-trust privacy model. We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with:

  • Government & Emergency Authorities: Real-time data, including GPS and telemetry, is shared directly with authorized public safety officials, dispatchers, and municipal departments to resolve tickets or emergencies.
  • Service Providers: Secure cloud infrastructure partners bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When mandated by law, court order, or to protect public safety.

6. Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures to protect your data, including cryptographic tokenization, end-to-end encryption in transit, and secure cloud archival. Our architecture ensures zero local caching of identity credentials, preventing unauthorized access even if the device is compromised.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law (e.g., maintaining tamper-proof audit trails for administrative accountability).

  • Data Deletion Rights: You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data. You can initiate this process directly within the App's account settings or by contacting our support team.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without verified parental consent. If we become aware that we have collected such data inadvertently, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. We will notify you of any material changes via an in-app notification or email prior to the changes taking effect.
